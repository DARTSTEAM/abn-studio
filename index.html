2026 · Retail · Sleep & Rest

Calm

Creative + High Visual AI · Campaign
The challenge

Hot Sale is the loudest week of the Argentine retail calendar — every brand screaming the same discounts into the same feed. Calm needed to cut through without simply shouting a bigger percentage off, and turn a mattress sale into something people actually wanted to watch and share.

The idea

We started from a universal truth: a bad night's sleep makes you short-tempered — the kind of day where anything sets you off. So we built the campaign around the split-second before you lose it, and swapped the Argentine swear word for the thing that would have prevented it: Calm. “Almohada”, “Colchón”, “Espuma”, “Cama” land exactly where the insult was meant to go. Four films — road rage (Autos), a rough morning (Mañana), the office (Oficina) and Tuesday-night football (Papi Fútbol) — plus a product reel, all in the visual language of the clips people already screenshot and forward.

The result

Produced with a hybrid-intelligence system — human comedic timing and casting judgment paired with AI-first execution. Five films delivered without a single shoot day, ready in time for Hot Sale.

2026 · Finance · Banking

Banco Galicia

Creative + High Visual AI · Campaign
The challenge

June 2026: the FIFA World Cup kicks off in the United States — the most-watched event on earth, and for a football nation like Argentina, far more than a tournament. Galicia wanted to own the cultural conversation at its most saturated moment, surrounded by competitors, through guerrilla marketing — without sponsoring AFA or the national team, without World Cup promos or discounts, and without raffling trips to the US. And it had to be conceived, developed and executed in under 14 days.

The idea

At a moment of peak national pride, we leaned on one absolute truth: Galicia is the only truly Argentine bank — the only one that operates exclusively inside the country. From there we built “Doble camiseta”: a distinctly Argentine way of unmasking the banks that keep a plan B and operate abroad, positioning Galicia as the one that only celebrates when Argentina wins. To carry it, we recreated an iconic scene from the classic film “Nueve Reinas”, shot-for-shot, rewriting the script around the concept.

The result

Insight, script and concept validated, the film was produced with a hybrid-intelligence system — human judgment paired with AI-first execution — crafted for hyperrealism, with a human voice-over carrying the emotional weight.
